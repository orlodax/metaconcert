STEP 0

Install

Copy two directories. Nothing else is touched.

.claude/
├── agents/
│   ├── mc-observer.md       intake
│   ├── mc-cartographer.md   map
│   ├── mc-diagnostician.md  hypothesise
│   ├── mc-historian.md      hypothesise
│   ├── mc-falsify.md        falsify
│   ├── mc-oracle.md         specify
│   ├── mc-builder.md        build
│   └── mc-adversary.md      attack
└── skills/metaconcert/
    └── SKILL.md             the director

Project scope or user scope

Either <repo>/.claude/ or ~/.claude/. The director globs both and project wins on a name collision, so a repo can override one member without forking the set.

It stays out of your way

The mc- prefix and the “never select proactively” clause in every description keep these out of normal routing. The skill is invocation: user, so it runs only when you call it.

STEP 1

Open a clean session

The easiest thing to get wrong, and fatal when you do.

Also wrong: briefing a coordinator agent first and letting it plan. You have pre-committed every downstream member to a frame. And a subagent cannot run this at all — triggers flow downward, a subagent has no slash-command interface, and an architect-as-director is the failure mode the design exists to avoid.

STEP 2

Invoke

No required format. Say it the way you would to a colleague.

/metaconcert i keep getting a 500 on retry uploads, only under load, and only since we moved to the new pool
/metaconcert work on issues/backend-defects.md
/metaconcert item 42 of issues/backend-defects.md

What the director does before spending anything

  • 1Resolves the reference: free prose is the brief; a path gets read; an ordinal gets resolved to a best candidate.
  • 2Echoes the referent back in one line — the entry’s own first line — and waits.
  • 3Never runs a full roster against a guess. If nothing resolves it asks once, plainly, and stops.

Watch for this

Ordinals are ambiguous: the 42nd checkbox, the 42nd numbered entry, and the entry labelled 42 are often three different things. Read the echo before you confirm.

A path plus prose: the file is the source, and your prose is your own framing — it goes straight to quarantine class A.

STEP 3

Answer the two questions

Asked once, together, so it is not two interruptions. Both answers go to no member.

The second question — your own estimate of the cost — is a separate artifact, human-estimate, and it is never folded into task-signature. A forecaster told what you expect has been anchored to the inside view its contract exists to refuse.

STEP 4

Approve the plan

Discovery runs first, always. You see the roster before anything is spent.

What the plan tells you

  • ·Members found, and which phase each claims
  • ·Phases with no member, classified (below)
  • ·Which existing project agents it proposes to borrow
  • ·For two members in one phase: whether they answer the same question (divergence is signal) or different ones merely run in parallel (outputs are not comparable and must not be presented as corroborating)

An empty phase is one of two things

  • !unstaffed — no member declares it. A defect in the roster, reported prominently and recorded in the ledger. Never a shrug.
  • ·deselected — a member exists and you declined it. A choice, recorded with your reason.

Either way the director states which bias is now uncontrolled, and never substitutes itself. ledger is exempt — it is director-owned and no member may claim it.

A member you authored in this session may appear in discovery but not yet be dispatchable — registration can lag by one turn. The plan says so rather than promising the phase is covered; if dispatch fails, retry on the next turn before concluding anything.

STEP 5

The run

Nine phases in a fixed order, and three points where it stops and asks you.

STEP 6

Your three decisions

If a run ever hands you a tidy consensus, it has failed silently.

◆ Divergence

You get: your quarantined instinct (or Mode B’s prior analysis) beside the independent ranking; every assumption I am supplying from the map, verbatim; the forecast against your own estimate.

You do: pick a hypothesis.

It refuses: to pick for you. Asked, it declines and restates the divergence.

Record no verdict here. Who was right is decided at falsify, by observation. A verdict formed at this stop is hindsight — the exact distortion the measurement routes around.

◆ Open questions

You get: the spec ambiguities the oracle listed instead of resolving.

You do: resolve them now, before anything is built.

It refuses: to pick an interpretation quietly. That is how a spec gap becomes a shipped bug.

◆ Reconcile

You get: blind-authored criteria beside the adversary’s findings, and separately the builder’s model-delta.

You do: read the gaps — they are the finding.

It refuses: to resolve them. This stop falls after attack on purpose: the adversary must not see the builder’s account of its own work.

STEP 7

Read a member’s output

Each member declares its own contract. Check the output against it — that is your job, not the director’s.

What the director is allowed to assemble — and what it is not

It mayIt may not
Build spec by verbatim selection from the decision and the relevant sections of system-map, each section carrying a one-line provenance tag. Write a substantive sentence with no source artifact. That is authorship, and it puts its own view into the one input the oracle must treat as given.
Derive task-signature from the structural content of symptom-brief — files touched, subsystems crossed, interface/migration/config changes, whether it spans repos. Supply an answer the brief is silent on. It records not stated instead.
Split the chosen hypothesis-ranking entry by field, once — the one enumerated exception. Summarise, rename or merge any artifact. Pass-through is verbatim.

The field split, in full: the statement and Predicts line may be appended to the decision; Test and Outcome map go to falsify only, relabelled under neutral tokens; Mechanism, Prior, Cost and the ordering are the diagnostician’s reasoning — you see them at the stop, nothing downstream does. Relabelling is the only edit permitted to a pre-registered test: not the command, not a path, not a threshold, not a word of the outcome map.

STEP 8

Troubleshooting

Four situations with a prescribed response. The first is the one that needs a picture.

The pre-registered test won’t execute

It may not be rewritten. Either this run keeps no falsify evidence — unresolved in Modes A and B — or you authorise a re-dispatch to the hypothesise member for a replacement, asked without disclosing that the hypothesis was chosen: “author an executable replacement test for H2”, never “H2 is the one we are going with”.

No member claims falsify

Do not skip it — that costs the protocol its only evidence. Report unstaffed, then borrow an agent, or let the director run the command and report raw output with no interpretation. Prefer the borrowed agent: a test the director runs is un-blinded by construction, since it holds both the label mapping and the quarantine. Record it as un-blinded and discount it.

The session compacted mid-run

Verbatim pass-through is silently broken and nothing downstream can detect it. Record the compaction as a phase defect. The same applies to any summarising you notice: the prose summary is precisely where the fluency illusion re-enters.

STEP 9

The ledger

Appended to .metaconcert/ledger.md. Without it this is a ritual, not a method.

Also recorded, every run

Date, domain, task, mode · members run · members deselected with your reason · wall-clock · per member, the size of the prompt the director built · your estimate against the forecast and both against the actual · map assumptions that proved load-bearing · specify-vs-attack gaps · findings by severity · the builder’s model-delta · any defect found later.

Phase defects: unstaffed phases · leaked phases with attempt counts · members that owed a CONTRACT CONFLICTS section and supplied none · borrowed agents · un-blinded falsify observations · any mid-run compaction.

Do not log a membrane claim as confirmed

A member stating it did not receive something is exactly the fluent, plausible, unfalsifiable claim this protocol exists to distrust. Convergent self-reports from members that share an ambient layer and carry identical instructions are one measurement taken N times.

No shipped member can read its own input-token count, and a borrowed agent’s grant may differ — check rather than assume. The only mechanical check is the size of the prompt the director itself constructed: a weak proxy, logged as such.

LIMITS

What the membrane cannot exclude

Operationally relevant, so it is here and not in a footnote: three channels reach a member that the director does not compose.

CeilingLever
1 · shared priorsIsolation decorrelates context, never training weights. Partial: run attack on a different model family.
2 · shared ambient layerIdentical in every member. None.
3 · forwarded parent stateCan neither be suppressed nor read back to see what it carried. None.
4 · member tool grantsGitignored is not unreadable, and removing the files was tried and defeated — the tree became unrepresentative and members correctly reported the absence as a defect. Between runs only: keep such material out of the tree.
5 · a director that holds everythingWithholding is mechanical once decided; the deciding is discipline, not structure. None inside a run.

All five mean the same thing operationally: member agreement is worth less than it looks, and weaker evidence than member divergence. Read the divergences first.

EXTEND

Adding a member, borrowing an agent

Drop a file in, it is adopted on the next run. Never edit the skill to add a member.

The template

---
name: mc-<name>
description: <one line>. ONLY use when
  explicitly invoked by the Metaconcert
  workflow. Never select proactively.
tools: Read, Grep, Glob
model: sonnet
---

## MEMBRANE

    phase:     <one of the nine>
    domain:    code | any | <yours>
    receives:  <artifact names>
    never:     <artifact names>
    produces:  <artifact name>
    model:     <why this model>

Holding Bash or Write means adding the CONTRACT PRECEDENCE block and listing ### CONTRACT CONFLICTS in the output format. A section demanded by the prose but missing from the format is one the member may silently omit.

Six rules for a member that earns its place

  • 1Name the bias. If you cannot say which failure mode it controls for, you built a helper, not a member.
  • 2never is the whole point.
  • 3Forbid the verdict. No “looks good”, no “seems correct” — enumerated evidence with citations.
  • 4Fix the output format exactly. Free-form prose is unauditable.
  • 5Minimum capability. A correctness property here, not just safety.
  • 6Two members in one phase run parallel and blind. That is how a second opinion is obtained.

Domain is a free string. A new domain needs at minimum a map member and an attack member — those two carry most of the load. Give every new artifact name one of the six kinds, and every receives name either a producer or a kind that explains its absence.

Borrowing an agent the repo already has

No modification needed — the membrane is controlled by what the director puts in front of it, not by the agent’s own definition. Prefix its prompt:

Operating as a Metaconcert member for this invocation.
Use only the material below. Do not request additional context.
Do not spawn subagents. Do not coordinate other agents.
Return only the artifact named in `produces`.
---
<material from the receiving member's receives list, verbatim>
  • okWorker-shaped agents — writers, implementers. Safe via call-site constraint.
  • !Coordinator-shaped agents — architects, leads. The information membrane holds; role drift does not, because their own system prompt still tells them to coordinate. Supply-only, watch for drift, and if it recurs build a thin mc- member. A real limitation, not a caveat.
  • ✕Never borrow an agent that will spawn its own subagents inside a phase: uncontrolled context flow re-enters and the run is void.
BEFORE YOU START

When not to run it

Almost always.

It is git bisect for reasoning

It costs many multiples of a normal session in tokens and in attention. It does not replace architect / builder / tester — that pattern optimises for information flowing freely downward, which is correct, and which this deliberately breaks.

Reach for it when the cheap loop has already failed twice, or when a silent error would be expensive. Architectural decisions and drafts failing for reasons you cannot name are the weaker case: with no causal hypothesis to falsify you pay in full for the map, the blind criteria and the adversarial review.

Run it as your default and you will hate it inside a week, abandon it, and lose the data. That is the realistic failure mode — not that it does not work.

Declared limits

  • ·The member/bias mapping is design intent, not measurement. Biases do not partition cleanly.
  • ·Invocation is selected on failure. Every run is drawn from problems pre-selected for your instinct having been wrong, which inflates both the divergence rate and the member-win rate. A control arm corrects only partially.
  • ·No efficacy data exists. Until the ledger has thirty entries with a control arm, this is a hypothesis with good ergonomics.

Partial runs are legitimate: state which phases were skipped and therefore which biases are uncontrolled. An honest partial beats a padded full run.
